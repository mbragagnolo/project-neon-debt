# Elite Scav: the brief

From `docs/art/cast.md` (approved 2026-09-06), in the game-designer's form.

## Role

Elite; teaches `gear`: a Scav that learned. Same silhouette and moveset,
faster, and no punish window, so the tutorial answer stops working and the
kit found so far has to carry. Guards the memory chip; rewards like a
miniboss; stays dead.

## Look

The Scav's body and rig, re-dressed. Coat and hood in black and concrete
grey, cleaner and better-fitting than any Scav's: he is paid. The pipe is a
red-painted rebar cutter with a lick of tape at the grip. The eye is
magenta, not amber: somebody else's money is behind it. Stands straighter
than a Scav in idle, hunches only to lunge.
