# Stitch: the brief

From `docs/art/cast.md` (approved 2026-09-06), in the game-designer's form.

## Role

Npc; the vendor and ripperdoc on the Mezz. Sells convenience and one gear
piece, never an unlock; installs the Sidewinder for free.

## Look

Broad, fifties, brown skin, shaved head. Surgical loupes pushed up onto the
forehead, a stained canvas apron over a green scrub top with the sleeves
gone, nitrile gloves. One arm is a plain working prosthetic, unpainted,
better made than anything he sells. A green work lamp on a gooseneck over
the stall is his light. He does not stand up; he sits behind the counter,
elbows on it, and leans in to talk. Green is a working machine.

## Silhouette

A wide seated block with the loupes on top.

## Colour

`green` / `green_d` scrub and lamp, `concrete2` / `concrete3` apron,
`skin2` family, `chrome_d` arm.

## Height

54 art px at 2x (108 screen px), seated.

## Rig parts

Head, torso, near arm, far arm. Seated: no legs. Four parts.

## Clips

`idle` (breathing, two or three poses), `talk`.

## Prop

None; the lamp is the room's.

## Voice

Dry, transactional, a surgeon who is also a pawnbroker. "Stitch says it'll
hold. Stitch says a lot of things."
