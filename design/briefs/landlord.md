# The Landlord: the brief

From `docs/art/cast.md` (approved 2026-09-06), in the game-designer's form.

## Role

The boss; teaches `damage`: every verb has a job and no verb is the answer.
A man in chrome; the Stacks never learned his name. Two phases; the second
calls two drones.

## Look

Tall, still, expensive. A long black coat with chrome piping at the collar
and cuffs, worn open over a fitted dark shirt. Both forearms and hands are
polished chrome, the only bright metal on anyone in the slice, and the
collections deck sits on his chest like a breastplate: a dark slab with a
magenta readout that is dim until he uses it. Pale, clean-shaven, hair
slicked back; no visor, no implants showing on the face, because he can
afford not to. The baton is a telescoping shock stick that lights magenta
along its length in the windup. Phase two: the deck's readout stays hot,
and the coat is off the shoulders and hanging from the belt so the chrome
arms read at full length. Magenta is money and the Landlord.

## Silhouette

The tallest thing in the game. The coat's line, the baton's length, the
deck's square.

## Colour

`black` / `black_l` coat and shirt, `chrome` / `chrome_d` arms and piping,
`magenta` / `magenta_d` deck and baton, `skin`, `hair`.

## Height

80 art px at 2x (160 screen px).

## Rig parts

Head, torso with deck, coat tails (two parts, so they swing), near arm
(upper, chrome forearm, hand), far arm, near leg (thigh, shin), far leg,
baton.

## Clips

`idle`, `run`, `windup`, `lunge`, `recover`, `stagger`, `slam_windup`,
`beam`, `phase`, `dead`; phase two is the set again under `p2_`.

## Prop

The telescoping shock baton, right hand, lit magenta in the windup.

## Voice

None in the slice; the ending card speaks for him.
