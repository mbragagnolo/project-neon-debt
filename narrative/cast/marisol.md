# Marisol

Speaker key `marisol`; `name.neighbour`. The 14th floor tenants'
association, which is her.

**Role.** The neighbour. The quest: she sends Dani into the Gut for Ferro's
chip and pays with his hardhat.

**Where.** The Mezz. Four pages the first time, one line while the quest is
open, two when the chip comes back, one after.

**Want.** For Ferro to have been a person. Not the chip's contents (she
never says what is on it); the chip itself, back from the people who would
repossess it with the rest of him. She will not go down there herself and
does not pretend she would.

**Voice.** Plain and unhurried, the only voice in the slice that speaks in
full sentences. Facts first, feelings as a clause ("He's dead down there.
I know that."). Practical about danger ("The water's live"). Gives the
reward as an instruction, not a gift ("Take it anyway."; "Don't die in
it."). Says the corp's name once, as the reason a thing is broken.

**Never says.** Please. A joke. Anything about Dani's debt.

**Sample lines.**
- `marisol.offer.3`: He's dead down there. I know that. I want the chip back before they repossess what's left of him.
- `marisol.complete.2`: The hat's yours. Don't die in it.
- `marisol.after`: Go on. The Landlord's got a floor to work, and it's ours.

**For the sheet.** Clips `idle` (4 frames) and `talk` (2 frames at 3 fps),
slower than Stitch's. Name tag magenta-pink (`COL_MARISOL`). Her longest
page is the longest line in the game at 1085 px of the box's 1296; the
sheet's talk clip runs the whole page, so it must loop clean.

**For the audio.** If voiced: mid-register, even, no rasp; the room's hum
under her.
