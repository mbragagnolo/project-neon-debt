# Stitch

Speaker key `stitch`; `name.vendor`. Stacks slang for a ripperdoc.

**Role.** Ripperdoc and pawn: the vendor, and the surgeon who installs the
Sidewinder. The reason the hub visit matters.

**Where.** The Mezz, at the stall. Met on the first visit (a greeting, then
the stall), when carrying the sealed Sidewinder (the install instead of the
stall), once after the install (one line, then the stall), then only the
stall.

**Want.** To stay in business in a district the corp wrote off. Credits
only; no favours, no questions about where a thing came from, no
warranties. Will cut anyone open who can pay, and says so.

**Voice.** A shopkeeper's patter, clipped. Lists ("Ripperdoc, pawn,
whatever the day needs"). Stops himself mid-question and refuses the
answer ("Where'd you-- no. Don't tell me."). The dry line is about the
bill ("The anaesthetic's on your tab."). Calls the corp by its name, as a
former supplier. Never warm; occasionally honest ("That's the trick of
it.").

**Never says.** Thank you. A price out loud (the stall does that). Anything
about the Landlord.

**Sample lines.**
- `stitch.greet.2`: Credits only. VESTA stopped taking my calls.
- `stitch.install.2`: Lie down. This won't hurt. The anaesthetic's on your tab.
- `stitch.after_install`: Legs feel wrong for a day. Then they feel like yours. That's the trick of it.

**For the sheet.** Clips `idle` (4 frames) and `talk` (2 frames at 4 fps),
as npc.gd's table has them; the talk is the hands, not the face. The name
tag is green (`COL_STITCH`): a working machine's colour, which is what he
is to the Stacks.

**For the audio.** If voiced: fast, nasal, a smoker's rasp; a page turn
sound for the stall opening.
