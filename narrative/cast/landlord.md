# The Landlord

Speaker key `landlord`; `name.boss`. A title, not a name: the Stacks never
learned his.

**Role.** The boss. The debt book's enforcer, working the towers floor by
floor; his deck carries the collections override that switches defaulters'
augs off. Beating him is stealing that.

**Where.** Collections, floor 40. He has one line in the slice, and it is a
toast, not a page: the phase shift calls the drones ("VESTA SUPPORT. TO
ME.", boss_phase_shift_state.gd). `bark.pre_boss` was written for the door
to his floor and nothing emits it.

**Want.** The account closed. Not cruelty, not money for himself: the
paperwork squared. He calls the corp's name as an employee calls the
office, and the drones come.

**Voice.** Institutional. Speaks in the corp's capitals, the way the
notices are printed; every line could be stamped. Never addresses Dani by
her name, only by the account. No jokes; the horror is that he is polite.

**Never says.** His name. Anything in the first person about himself. A
threat (the notice already made it).

**Sample lines.**
- `notice.repossession`: the notice on 14-C's door is his voice on paper.
- the phase-shift toast, "VESTA SUPPORT. TO ME." (in code, not the table)
- `bark.pre_boss` is Dani's line about him: He's got the override. I've got a wrench.

**For the sheet.** No talk clip; he never uses the dialogue box. The magenta
readout on the deck is his colour for any name tag or subtitle.

**For the audio.** If voiced: processed, a tannoy, a comm's compression;
the deck's magenta readout hums when he speaks.
