# Dani Okonkwo

Speaker key `dani`. The corp prints her `OKONKWO, D.`, account 14C-0091-77
(`name.protagonist`, `name.account`); the bark box prints `DANI`. The look
is `docs/art/cast.md`; this is the voice.

**Role.** The player. Behind on payments, not a hero. Her body was rebuilt
on a standard finance plan and she is upside down on the loan; the
Landlord has reached her floor.

**Where.** Every room. She speaks only on the bark box, one line at a story
beat, the game not paused: twelve lines in the whole slice.

**Want.** To keep the body she is living in. Not freedom, not forgiveness:
time. She will steal the keys before she will pay for them, and she will
not ask anyone for help she cannot pay back.

**Voice.** Terse, tired, working-class wry. One or two short sentences; the
second one turns the first ("Their terminal. Their body. Every save is a
receipt."). Never a question. The joke is the debt, and she never laughs at
it. Names the corp by its short name, like a landlord's name. Swears never;
the paperwork is the swearing.

**Never says.** Anything heroic. Anything about the city outside the Stacks.
More than two sentences.

**Sample lines.**
- `bark.first_save`: Their terminal. Their body. Every save is a receipt.
- `bark.overload`: Corporate property, pointed the other way.
- `bark.post_boss`: It's not forgiveness. It's the keys.

**For the sheet.** No talk clip: she is never on the dialogue box. The
cyan piping and the magenta visor are the box's colours for her name
(`COL_NAME` in bark_box.gd is the visor's cyan-white).

**For the audio.** If voiced: low, flat, a little hoarse, no rise at the
end of a line. The bark box plays nothing today; a short UI tick would do.
