# Ferro

Speaker key none; `name.defaulter`. He has no lines.

**Role.** The defaulter who ran. The body in the Gut the quest is about;
the chip is what he did not want repossessed with the rest of him. The
reward is his hardhat, the best helmet in the game.

**Where.** The sump, dead. A sign and a chip.

**Want.** Was: to keep one thing they could not take. He took it down into
live water rather than let Collections have it. The want outlives him and
becomes Marisol's.

**Voice.** None, and that is the rule: he is spoken about, never for. Every
line about him is someone else's ("Ferro ran when Collections hit twelve";
"he'd rather die down here than let them take it"). A note in his hand
would break it.

**Never says.** Anything.

**Sample lines.** (said about him)
- `marisol.offer.2`
- `bark.chip`
- `marisol.complete.1`

**For the sheet.** A body prop (`assets/props/body.png`), not a character:
no clips. The hardhat is the item's icon.

**For the audio.** The sump's water, live; nothing of his own.
